Method

Every asset was submitted to the HumanStandard detection API, which returns a three-valued verdict — AI, human, or uncertain — at three calibrated operating points with their false-positive rates published. Assets are tiered on those operating points rather than on thresholds chosen here: suspect when HumanStandard's human-safe tier (~1-2% FPR) says ai | clean when its recall tier (~5-10% FPR) says human | contested when the tiers disagree. Where the operating points disagree, the recording is borderline by the detector's own account and is routed to a person. Anything below the detector's confidence floor is routed to review regardless of verdict, and anything that could not be scored is excluded from the clean base rather than assumed safe. Suspect revenue is escrowed in full; contested revenue is weighted at 50% because uncertainty is not a finding.
